		\begin{quote}
			All forms of private hoarding of SCREW-NUTS---including such usages as ornamentation, holding for sentimental value, or otherwise tertiary application---shall be forbidden. All Animals are hereby ORDERED TO DELIVER, on or before the FIRST SNOWFALL, to this GLADE OF REPRESENTATIVES, or to me, or to any crow directly in my service, all SCREW-NUTS now owned by them, or SCREW-NUTS coming into their possession. Upon delivery, the SCREW-NUTS shall be redeemed for an equal worth in LEAVES. Failure to comply to this Order shall, if deemed unintentional, result in a FINE not exceeding FIVE LEAVES; however, if deemed wilful, the subject shall be sentenced to DEATH BY STOAT.
		\end{quote}
